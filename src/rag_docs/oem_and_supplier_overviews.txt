Valeo Park4U (supplier overview):
- Design intent (paraphrase): Assist/automate the parking maneuver by detecting suitable spaces and controlling steering (and sometimes speed/braking depending on configuration).
- Key interactions (paraphrase): Driver initiates feature; system performs maneuver; driver supervises and can stop.
- RAG cues: “space detection”, “low-speed maneuvering”, “driver supervision”, “controls steering/brake”.

Hyundai Mobis RSPA 2 / parking control (press + industry writeups):
- Key technical idea (paraphrase): Sensor fusion (surround cameras + ultrasonic sensors) plus parking line recognition enables autonomous parking even when there are no adjacent vehicles.
- RAG cues: “sensor fusion”, “parking line recognition”, “empty parking space”, “remote parking”.

General OEM manual patterns (Autopark / APA / Remote Parking):
- Common engagement conditions (generalized):
  • very low speed; sufficient sensor visibility; clear parking lines or space geometry; driver confirmation; (remote) key-fob/phone link present.
- Common cancellation / abort:
  • driver overrides steering/brake; obstacle too close; system detects uncertainty; sensor blocked; system fault; comms loss.
- Typical warnings (generalized):
  • Driver is responsible; do not rely on system to detect every object; stay alert and ready to stop.

Mapping to your APA functions:
1) Provide braking force / 8) Apply emergency braking:
   - deviation themes: delayed braking, insufficient pressure, false braking, brake not applied.
2) Control steering angle:
   - deviation themes: wrong curvature, oscillation, actuator saturation, no steer response.
3) Control longitudinal motion:
   - deviation themes: unintended acceleration, no motion when commanded, overshoot stop, creep.
4) Detect obstacles:
   - deviation themes: missed detection, false positives, classification error, latency, occlusion.
5) Activate mode:
   - deviation themes: activates in unsafe context, wrong mode, wrong space.
6) Deactivate mode:
   - deviation themes: fails to disengage, disengages unexpectedly, inconsistent HMI.
7) Allow driver override:
   - deviation themes: override delayed/ignored, re-engagement after override, mode confusion.
9) Disable upon fault detection:
   - deviation themes: fault not detected, detected too late, unsafe degradation path.


=== DETAILED SUPPLIER SPECIFICATIONS ===

Continental APA (Automated Parking Assist):
  Sensor configuration: 12 ultrasonic sensors (40 kHz, 0.15-5.0 m range) plus
  4 fisheye surround-view cameras (190-degree FOV each, 1280x960 resolution).
  Communication: CAN/CAN-FD interface to vehicle gateway. Sensor ASIL rating:
  ASIL-B per ISO 26262. Maximum operating speed 10 km/h (hard-limited by ECU).
  Parking space detection: minimum space length 1.1x vehicle length for
  perpendicular, 1.3x for parallel. Space width minimum 2.3 m. System measures
  space dimensions during drive-by at <30 km/h using ultrasonic scan.
  Path planning: geometric planning with up to 7 correction maneuvers. Abort
  conditions: obstacle detected within 30 cm clearance zone, steering angle
  tracking error >5 degrees, wheel speed inconsistency between ABS and
  odometry, completion time exceeding 120 seconds.
  Known limitations: performance degrades in heavy rain (ultrasonic false
  echoes from water spray). Fisheye camera stitching artifacts near pillars
  and tall obstacles at view boundaries. System does not detect overhead
  obstacles (height barriers, low ceilings below 2.0 m).

HL Mando IEB (Integrated Electronic Brake):
  Part number: 58520-XA000 (Kia EV9 application). Maximum system pressure
  180 bar. Pressure build time: zero to 100 bar in <150 ms. Motor: brushless
  DC with ball screw actuator. Dual-circuit hydraulic output (front/rear split).
  NHTSA Recall 24V-693 (January 2024): affected Kia EV9 vehicles equipped with
  HL Mando IEB units. Failure mode: repeated brake applications at speeds below
  approximately 2 mph (3.2 km/h) could result in insufficient braking
  performance. Root cause: ball screw mechanism did not fully advance during
  rapid low-pressure cycling, producing 20-40% pressure deficit. Remedy:
  software update to ECU brake apply algorithm. Affected approximately 7,600
  vehicles in the US market.
  Operating temperature range: -40C to +85C (ECU), -40C to +120C (hydraulic
  unit). Failure detection: internal pressure sensor cross-checked with motor
  position encoder. Fault response: revert to manual pedal-push-through mode
  (no power assist, driver applies direct mechanical force).

Bosch AVP (Automated Valet Parking) — SAE Level 4 in facility:
  System classification: Type 2 AVP per VDA standard — infrastructure-assisted
  automated driving within equipped parking facility. Maximum vehicle speed
  6 km/h within facility, controlled by facility management system.
  Architecture: dual-channel monitoring — onboard vehicle safety system plus
  infrastructure safety monitoring (overhead cameras, LiDAR at facility
  choke points). Watchdog timeout 500 ms — if either channel does not confirm
  safe state within 500 ms, minimum risk maneuver activates (immediate stop
  plus EPB engagement).
  Infrastructure requirements: facility must be surveyed and digitally mapped.
  UWB positioning anchors at <20 m spacing. Infrastructure perception covers
  all driving corridors — no vehicle-only perception zones. Communication via
  facility Wi-Fi (5 GHz, <50 ms latency requirement) or dedicated V2I link.
  Known limitations: system cannot operate if facility infrastructure is
  offline (no fallback to vehicle-only mode). Maximum facility size limited by
  positioning infrastructure coverage. Vehicle must support drive-by-wire
  interface (steering, braking, propulsion) compatible with Bosch AVP protocol.

Valeo Park4U Gen 3 (Automated Parking):
  Path planning: Hybrid A* algorithm with kinematic vehicle model. Planning
  horizon: full maneuver computed before execution begins. Clearance margins:
  0.8 m lateral for parallel parking, 0.6 m lateral for perpendicular parking.
  Minimum detectable space: parallel 1.2x vehicle length, perpendicular space
  width 2.3 m. Maximum correction maneuvers: 5 (perpendicular) or 7 (parallel).
  Abort conditions: obstacle enters clearance zone (<20 cm), path tracking
  error exceeds 15 cm lateral or 10 degrees heading, ultrasonic sensor fault
  on more than 2 sensors simultaneously, completion time exceeds 90 seconds.
  Sensor suite: 12 ultrasonic (50 kHz) + 4 fisheye cameras. Optional: 1 rear
  LiDAR for enhanced obstacle detection. Ultrasonic scan rate: 15 Hz per
  sensor (time-division multiplexed). Camera frame rate: 30 fps.
  Integration: communicates with vehicle via private CAN bus to EPS, IEB, and
  powertrain ECU. Requires steering angle feedback <10 ms latency.

Hyundai-Kia RSPA (Remote Smart Parking Assist):
  Activation: key fob button hold (continuous press required) or smartphone
  app with Bluetooth connection to vehicle. Maximum operating speed 5 mph
  (8 km/h), hard-limited. Operating range: key fob must remain within 6 m of
  vehicle (Bluetooth range with required signal strength). Smartphone control
  adds approximately 100-300 ms latency versus key fob.
  Safety: system cancels immediately if key fob button is released (dead-man
  switch). Cancellation response time <200 ms (brake apply initiation).
  Ultrasonic monitoring active during entire maneuver with 20 cm minimum
  clearance threshold.
  NHTSA Recall 23V-446 (2023): affected Hyundai Ioniq 5 and Kia EV6 vehicles
  with RSPA feature. Approximately 12,400 vehicles. Failure mode: RSPA could
  activate unintentionally and move the vehicle without driver command under
  specific key fob radio frequency interference conditions. Remedy: software
  update to add additional activation confirmation requirements.
  Known limitations: does not operate in multi-story structures (ramp grade
  detection triggers abort). Maximum maneuver distance 10 m from starting
  position. Requires clear line-of-sight between key fob and vehicle for
  Bluetooth connection stability.

BMW Remote Parking (including Remote Theft Vehicle Recovery):
  Activation: BMW Display Key or My BMW smartphone app. Key fob operating
  range: up to 300 m line-of-sight for remote parking monitoring (actual
  parking control limited to 6 m range, same Bluetooth constraint). Maximum
  vehicle speed 6 km/h during remote parking.
  Sensor configuration: 12 ultrasonic + 4 surround-view cameras + 1 front
  radar. Surround-view provides bird's-eye visualization on key fob display.
  System features: forward and reverse straight-line parking (garage entry/
  exit), perpendicular space entry/exit. Does not support parallel parking in
  remote mode. Maximum maneuver length: 10 m forward or reverse.
  Safety: dead-man switch on key fob (button release triggers immediate stop).
  Ultrasonic emergency stop at 25 cm obstacle distance. Camera-based
  pedestrian detection adds secondary emergency stop layer. System pauses
  if any sensor reports fault condition.
  Known limitations: key fob display has limited resolution (320x240), making
  remote monitoring of tight clearances difficult. Smartphone app depends on
  cellular connection for camera stream, adding 200-800 ms video latency.
  System does not support angled or non-standard parking space geometries.
